StatsPro

Analyze your thesis or assignment data without coding. Upload a CSV, get the right statistical tests, plain-English explanations, charts and an APA-style write-up you can check and reuse.

Analyze your dataset, free → View on GitHub

The app runs on Streamlit Community Cloud. If it was idle it may take up to a minute to wake.

Problems it solves

"I don't understand my data"

Instant summary, data-quality score and charts for any CSV.

"My data has missing values"

Automatic cleaning with every choice logged, so you can report it.

"Which test should I use?"

The Assumption Doctor picks the right test and explains why.

"What does this p-value mean?"

Plain-English results with effect sizes and multiple-testing correction.

"I need the Results section"

APA-style Methods and Results text, plus a PDF report.

"Can I reproduce this later?"

A script that recomputes every number, and an Integrity Seal that ties the report to your data.

Your data

StatsPro does not save your files. An uploaded CSV is processed in memory for your session only and is gone when you close the tab or the session ends. It is never written to a database. The app is hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud. For sensitive or confidential data, run StatsPro on your own computer (instructions below). Automated suggestions are a starting point, not a substitute for your supervisor's judgment.

Everything inside

Preprocessing

KNN imputation, IQR outlier handling, duplicate and constant-column removal, quality score.

Statistical tests

Scale-aware normality, correlations with CIs, chi-square with Cramér's V, ANOVA with effect sizes, VIF, regression, FDR correction.

Assumption Doctor

Checks the assumptions behind every test, switches to a safer one when they fail, and explains why.

Thesis-ready write-up

APA-style Methods and Results text plus a script that reproduces every number.

Trust Report

Prediction intervals with measured coverage, fairness by group, and too-good-to-be-true warnings.

Integrity Seal

Every PDF ends with a verifiable fingerprint of your data and an honest ledger of how many findings may be chance.

ML Studio

Six models with cross-validation, permutation importance, diagnostics, live and batch prediction, model export.

Reports

AI-generated narrative insights and a downloadable PDF report.

Run it on your own machine

# or with Docker:
